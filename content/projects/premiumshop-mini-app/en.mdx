## How It Started

Back then, Telegram mini-apps had just started trending. The most popular one was **Hamster Kombat**, which everyone seemed to be playing.  
Around that time, one of my clients—who was selling Telegram Stars and Premium subscriptions—reached out and said:  
“I want a mini-app for this. Keep it simple, but make sure the UX feels professional.”  

Since I love working on fresh and trendy projects, I accepted right away.  

## How It Went

For the UI, I decided to take inspiration from a well-known platform in this space, since it’s one of the best examples in terms of both UI and UX.  
The project stack included: **Next.js, TypeScript, React Hook Form, TanStack Query, Tailwind, and Zod** (plus a few smaller tools).  

### Key Features of the Mini-App
- Buy Telegram Stars  
- Purchase Premium subscription  
- Wallet-only payment system  
- View purchase history  
- User authentication  
- Phone number verification via OTP  

One cool feature was that it also pulled user info directly from Telegram—like profile photo and name—which made the experience even more engaging.
