## How Nightwatch Solved the Problem of Missing Important Messages on Telegram

### Where Did the Story Begin?  
For many of us, Telegram is more than just a messenger;  
it’s a whole world of groups, channels, and valuable content we interact with every day.  
But there’s always one big problem:  
**too many messages make important ones disappear in the crowd.**  

I’m sure this has happened to you too:  
Someone in your work group or favorite channel shares something crucial,  
but when you check back a few hours later, you have to scroll through hundreds of messages to find it.  

**That’s when I decided to build my own solution.**

### The Spark of the Idea  
I kept thinking:  
*“I wish there was someone always watching the chats 24/7, filtering only the things that mattered to me and putting them right in front of me…”*  

And that’s how the idea of **Nightwatch Bot** was born.  

> Nightwatch is like a night guard—always awake to make sure you never miss an important message. 🌙✨  

### What Does Nightwatch Do?  
It’s super simple:  
- Add the bot to your groups and channels.  
- Give it a list of **keywords** (like: “hiring”, “update”, “meeting”).  
- From that moment on, whenever a message contains one of those keywords, it will be **automatically forwarded to you**.  

In plain words:  
You don’t have to read everything anymore.  
Only the important ones come to you.  

### The Experience  
When I first built and tested Nightwatch, it felt like having an assistant that knew exactly what I was looking for.  
For example, in a group of 1,000 people, someone might drop the word *“article”* once.  
Instead of digging through countless stickers and small talk, the bot instantly forwards it to me.  

That feeling of being back in control was priceless.
