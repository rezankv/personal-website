## Project Overview

After building a Telegram Mini App for selling Telegram Premium subscriptions and Stars <Link href={routes.SINGLE_PROJECT_ROUTE("premiumshop-mini-app")}>(+)</Link>, the client decided to develop a full web version of the product to increase sales and improve visibility on Google.
The web version provides the same core features as the Mini App, but with several important updates and enhancements.

## Project Goals

The web version was developed with the following objectives:

- Make the service accessible to users outside Telegram
- Improve SEO and increase organic traffic 
- Boost conversion rates and overall sales

## Key Improvements in the Web Version

1. **New Authentication System**  
   In the previous version, authentication was handled through Telegram Login.
   In the web version, users must register using their email and create an account, making the service independent of Telegram and accessible to a wider audience. 
2. **Additional Standard E-commerce Pages**  
   To make the website more complete and professional, several essential pages were added, including: 
   - About Us 
   - Terms & Conditions 
   - Privacy Policy 
   - FAQs and other informational pages 
3. **Enhanced UI/UX**  
   The design has been improved to look cleaner, more modern, and more user-friendly compared to the Mini App version.

## Final Result

After a few weeks of development, the web version was successfully launched and received positive feedback, which was both exciting and satisfying for the team.
