Over the years that I’ve been programming, I’ve come to realize that no matter what field we work in, there are some fundamental principles we all need to know in order to write clean, maintainable, and scalable code.

In this series of posts, I’ll walk you through those principles. Since I know everyone’s technical background is different, I’ll start from the very basics so that anyone can follow along and benefit.

I hope you find these posts helpful and that they contribute to improving your technical skills.
