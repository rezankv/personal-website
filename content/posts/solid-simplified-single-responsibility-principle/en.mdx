### Introduction
In the world of software development, writing code that “just works” is not enough. Applications that become increasingly complex, buggy, and difficult to maintain as they grow often suffer from poor design practices. One of the most important sets of principles that helps us write clean, scalable, and maintainable code is the SOLID principles.

SOLID is a collection of five principles introduced by <Link href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_C._Martin" target="_blank">Robert C. Martin</Link>. These principles help us eliminate improper dependencies, make changes without breaking other parts of the system, and avoid unnecessary architectural complexity. Following these principles is especially valuable in long-term and team-based projects, making software development a more enjoyable and reliable experience.
